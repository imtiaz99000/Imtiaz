## ⭐ Featured work: RSSP Business Tracker

A daily performance website that my team of 100+ checks every morning, built and run by me.

Every morning I used to download a raw activation export of tens of thousands of rows, rebuild an Excel dashboard by hand and paste screenshots into WhatsApp. Now one upload turns the raw Activation, SIM stock, bike distance, staff and attendance exports into live KPIs.

- Leaderboards, RSSP detail pages, supervisor scorecards and non performer reports
- SIM stock tracker with zero stock and FIFO lists, plus a bike compliance tracker
- Morning Brief with an action list and a weekly digest shared on WhatsApp in one tap
- Reports export as 8K pictures and Excel files, in Bangla and English
- Targets and personal data stay in private admin views

The live tracker holds company data, so it stays private. The demo runs the same app on made-up data.

👉 **[Try the live demo](https://imtiazkarim.com/rssp-demo/)**

**Built with:** HTML, CSS, JavaScript, Node.js serverless functions on Vercel, Vercel Blob storage, SheetJS for Excel, Playwright for testing, and Claude as my AI coding partner.
